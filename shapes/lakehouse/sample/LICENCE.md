# Licence of the sample

`ghcn_daily_sample.csv` holds 156 rows of NOAA's Global Historical Climatology Network Daily (GHCN-Daily), taken
from the course preview of its Canada cut (`preview/ghcn/`, GHCNd version 3.35, built 2026-11-07) by
`../extract_sample.py`. Source: `s3://noaa-ghcn-pds`, registry page https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-ghcn/.
